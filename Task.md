# Jets & Partners Front-End Challenge

## Objective
Rebuild one of the following Jets & Partners marketing pages inside a brand-new Next.js 14 project (App Router):
- Home
- About
- Empty-Legs
- Services

Match the visual design, layout, and interactions as closely as possible. Faithful replicas earn extra credit, but partial implementations are considered as long as the core layout is present.

## Requirements
1. Scaffold a fresh Next.js 14 project (TypeScript + App Router recommended).
2. Recreate the chosen page with responsive behavior for desktop, tablet, and mobile breakpoints.
3. Use semantic HTML, accessible components (ARIA roles, focus states, alt text), and meaningful typography.
4. Fetch or author content as needed. Images may be scraped from the live site or replaced with placeholders that respect the layout.
5. Keep styles organized (CSS Modules, Tailwind, or another structured approach).
6. Do not include external UI kits that would trivialize the layout; utility libraries (Framer Motion, Swiper, etc.) are acceptable when justified.
7. Document assumptions or shortcuts in `NOTES.md` at the project root if something cannot be completed.

## Submission Workflow
1. Fork `https://github.com/jets-partners-dev/NextJS-Front-End-Dev-Test`.
2. Create a branch named exactly `Full-Name/City-of-residency` (example: `Ahmed-Ali/Casablanca`).
3. Build your solution inside a `nextjs-app/[full-name]` folder (or similar) within your branch.
4. Commit early and often with clear messages.
5. Push the branch to your fork and open a pull request back to `main` on this repository.

## Timeline
- You have **24 hours** from the moment you receive the invitation email to submit your pull request.
- Late submissions may still be reviewed but will be marked accordingly.

## Evaluation Criteria
- **Visual accuracy & responsiveness** – fidelity to the selected page across breakpoints.
- **UX & polish** – transitions, hover states, scrolling behavior, and overall finish.
- **Code quality** – clarity, structure, reusability, and adherence to Next.js best practices.
- **Accessibility & semantics** – keyboard support, readable markup, and alt text.
- **Documentation** – concise explanation of decisions, trade-offs, or limitations.

## Allowed Resources
- You may reference Jets & Partners public pages and assets.
- AI tools are allowed but relying primarily on your own implementation increases the chance of moving forward.
- Any third-party libraries must be declared in your README or project notes.

## Deliverables Checklist
- [ ] Next.js 14 project with the selected page implemented.
- [ ] Responsive layout and accessible components.
- [ ] README inside your project explaining how to run/build it.
- [ ] Optional `NOTES.md` for decisions or follow-up ideas.
- [ ] Pull request opened within 24 hours following the instructions above.

Good luck, and thanks for investing the effort to show us how you build beautiful, thoughtful UIs!
